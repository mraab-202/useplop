FAQ

Questions employers ask.

Who sees my post?

Students at the entrepreneurship centers on the Plop network. Each center runs a branded board for its own community and sends email digests to students who subscribed. Your post goes to each center for review and, once approved, appears on that board and in its digests.

Does every center show my post?

No. Each center’s admin decides what fits their students, so a role that suits one campus may be passed over by another. That review is why students trust the board, and it is what makes the applications you do get worth having.

What can I post?

Jobs and internships, full-time or part-time, remote or on-site. If you run a competition, grant, accelerator, or startup credits program, you can post those too; they are always free.

What does it cost?

Job posts from employers are $199 for 30 days across the whole network, billed by card only after Plop approves your post. Internships, competitions, grants, accelerators, startup credits, and posts from universities, nonprofits, and student founders are free.

How do students apply?

However you choose: a link to your careers page or application form, or an email address. Plop does not sit between you and the applicant. Students see your name and organization as the poster.

How long does a post stay up?

Until the deadline or expiration date you set when posting. Boards remove expired posts automatically, so students never apply to roles that have closed.

A note for centers: community posts, student startups, competitions, grants, and accelerators stay free on Plop, and every post still goes through your approval queue. Employer posting exists so your students see real roles at companies that want to hire them.
