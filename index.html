FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is Plop?

Plop is a custom-branded opportunities board built for university entrepreneurship centers. It gives students one place to find jobs, internships, startup competitions, grants, accelerators, and startup credits. Each center gets its own branded subdomain (e.g., youruniversity.useplop.com) with their logo and colors.

Who is Plop for?

Plop is designed for university entrepreneurship centers, innovation hubs, incubators, and accelerators that want a single place to share opportunities with their students — instead of letting them disappear into Slack channels and newsletters.

Does my board start empty?

No. Plop curates national opportunities — startup competitions, accelerators, grants, credits, and student-relevant jobs — and delivers them to every board’s approval queue. You approve what fits your community, so your board is useful from day one.

How much does Plop cost?

Plop is free for the first 30 days — no credit card required. After that, it’s $250 per year (about $21 per month). This includes everything: custom branding, the curated opportunity feed, email subscriptions, admin dashboard, and unlimited posts. No hidden fees or tiered plans.

How long does it take to set up?

Less than 2 minutes. Sign up, add your logo and colors, and share your custom URL with your community. Your board arrives with current national opportunities already waiting in your approval queue.

What types of opportunities can be posted?

Jobs, internships, startup competitions, grants, accelerators, and startup credits. Anyone can submit an opportunity through your board, and admins approve submissions with one click before they go live. Rolling deadlines are supported for always-open opportunities.

Can students get notified about new opportunities?

Yes. Students subscribe to the categories they care about and choose instant, daily, or weekly email digests — so they never miss a deadline.
